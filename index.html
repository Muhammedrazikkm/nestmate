OUR CORE VISION

The Open Utility Manifesto

Everyday utility tools should be as friction-free and accessible as a pencil. Here is the pledge that governs every tool built under the Nestmate Group umbrella:

01

Zero Friction Access

We believe nobody should have to surrender their email address, verify a phone number, or remember a password just to compute an EMI, generate an invoice, or make a Wi-Fi barcode.

02

Privacy by Physics

The most secure database is the one that does not exist. We do not track your invoices, your client lists, or your calculations. Your data resides exclusively in your browser RAM and local device cache.

03

Commercial-Grade Precision

Free does not mean compromised. Every tool is engineered to commercial standards: multi-page PDF rendering, vector SVG scalability, and strict mathematical precision down to sub-cent rounding.

04

No Bait & Switch

We will never allow "3 free exports, then $19/mo". Everything released under Nestmate is 100% free forever, without artificial throttles, banner traps, or predatory trial expiration dates.

Privacy & Advertising Policy

1. Client-Side Utility Privacy

All core computational and generation tools on Nestmate (including QuoteCraft, QR Studio, and OmniCalc) operate entirely client-side inside your web browser. We do not store, collect, or transmit your calculation records, quotations, client names, or barcodes to any external database.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings or via aboutads.info.

3. Data Protection & GDPR / CCPA Compliance

Nestmate respects global privacy standards. Because our tools function locally on-device, zero sensitive business or personal documents are retained on our servers. For any inquiries, reach our team at nestmategroup@gmail.com.
