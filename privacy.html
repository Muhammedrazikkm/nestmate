Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last Updated & Verified: November 17, 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Summary: Nestmate is engineered around a "Privacy by Physics" philosophy. All utility functions (QuoteCraft calculations, QR code rendering, and OmniCalc computations) run strictly within your local browser sandbox. We never store, collect, transmit, or inspect your financial numbers, client invoices, or QR content on remote servers.

1. Introduction & Ownership

Welcome to Nestmate Group ("Nestmate", "we", "our", or "us"), available at https://www.nestmategroup.online. We are dedicated to delivering 100% free, accessible, and privacy-first web utilities to individuals, freelancers, and businesses across the globe.

This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when visiting our website, our strict zero-retention architecture, and how third-party advertising partners (including Google AdSense) operate cookies on this site.

2. Client-Side Utility Architecture (Zero Data Retention)

Unlike conventional web applications that upload your inputs to cloud databases, Nestmate tools operate 100% client-side via modern JavaScript and Web APIs:

  • QuoteCraft: Your quotation records, line items, customer addresses, logo images, and GST/VAT tax figures remain exclusively in your browser's local memory (`localStorage`). They are never transferred across our network.
  • QR Studio: Barcodes and QR codes are generated directly onto an HTML5 `` element using local client-side algorithms. Wi-Fi passwords, contact cards, and URLs are never logged.
  • OmniCalc: All mathematical formulas (EMI loan calculations, currency rates, scientific units) execute in real-time inside your CPU.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Disclosures

To keep Nestmate 100% free without paywalls, subscriptions, or gated logins, we display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks, primarily Google AdSense.

Google requires publishers to disclose the following information regarding advertising cookies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites across the World Wide Web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google's Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative consumer opt-out page at www.aboutads.info/choices.

4. Cookies & Web Beacons

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by websites that you visit. Nestmate does not use first-party tracking cookies or profiling cookies. However, third-party services on our site may deploy cookies:

  • Google AdSense & DoubleClick: Google uses cookies to serve ads, prevent click fraud, cap ad frequency, and report aggregate performance metrics.
  • Local Preferences: We use your browser's `localStorage` solely to remember your chosen theme (Dark vs. Light mode) and your saved QuoteCraft drafts on your own device.

5. Log Files & Analytics

Like almost all hosting infrastructure, our cloud hosting provider (Vercel Inc.) maintains standard automated server logs for security, DDOS prevention, and bandwidth monitoring. These logs may record standard protocol details such as Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of requests. None of these server logs are linked to personally identifiable information.

6. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Compliance

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific data protection rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because Nestmate does not collect or process personal data on servers, we hold no user profiles, email databases, or identifiable user dossiers. If you have questions regarding your GDPR rights, or if you contact us via email, you have the right to request access, correction, or erasure of your email correspondence.

7. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have rights regarding their personal information:

  • We do not sell personal information to third parties.
  • We do not collect personal identifying information through our tools.
  • Third-party advertising partners collect browsing cookies as described above. You may exercise your right to opt out of interest-based ad tracking through the opt-out links provided in Section 3.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Nestmate does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Our utility tools are intended for general audience use. If a parent or guardian believes that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13 via email contact, please reach out to us immediately, and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological, operational, or legal requirements. When changes are published, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be revised accordingly.
