What is QuoteCraft? (Your Free Online Quotation Maker)

QuoteCraft is a 100% free online quotation maker and commercial invoice generator developed by Nestmate Group. Engineered specifically for independent freelancers, small agency founders, contractors, and global vendors, it transforms commercial estimate creation into an instant, client-side experience requiring zero account sign-ups, zero subscriptions, and zero paywalls.

Unlike proprietary accounting platforms that watermark your documents or demand paid subscriptions for PDF downloads, QuoteCraft produces vector-sharp, professional PDF quotations with clean typography, automatic GST/VAT tax breakdowns, and custom company branding.

Key Invoicing & Quotation Maker Capabilities

1. Automated Multi-Tier Tax Engine

Statutory tax regulations require transparent distinction between base item costs, discounts, and regional value-added levies. QuoteCraft features a built-in mathematical engine capable of calculating:

  • Goods and Services Tax (GST): Full support for split Central/State (CGST/SGST) and Integrated (IGST) computations for Indian commercial invoices.
  • Value-Added Tax (VAT): Standard EU and UK VAT calculation schemes with inclusive or exclusive rate toggling.
  • Itemized & Lump-sum Discounts: Percentage-based or fixed amount deductions applied cleanly before tax calculation.

2. 14+ Curated Design Templates

Depending on your market niche, QuoteCraft provides distinct aesthetic themes:

  • Corporate Minimalist: Crisp monochrome tables suited for enterprise legal and consulting bids.
  • Creative Studio: Contemporary typographic balance with vibrant accent hues for design, marketing, and software engineering.
  • Construction & Trade: Detailed line-item breakdown accommodating material quantities, labor hours, and phase milestone payment schedules.

3. Total Client-Side Data Privacy

Commercial quotation data often contains sensitive billing rates, trade secrets, and customer names. Because QuoteCraft executes in your browser's V8 engine, your numbers never leave your laptop or smartphone. Local drafts are securely cached in your device's browser memory via the HTML5 Web Storage API.

Step-by-Step: How to Make a Quotation or Invoice Online for Free

  1. Open the Live Quotation Maker: Navigate to the QuoteCraft Interactive Workspace or visit quotecraft.nestmategroup.online.
  2. Set Company Credentials: Input your business name, address, tax identification (e.g. GSTIN / VAT registration number), and optionally upload your company logo JPEG or PNG.
  3. Add Client Specifications: Enter the recipient's organization name, contact address, and issued quotation date.
  4. Populate Billable Line Items: Define deliverables, unit quantities, and base rates. QuoteCraft automatically calculates item subtotals, discounts, and regional taxes in real-time.
  5. Define Payment & Commercial Terms: Choose payment validity period (e.g., Net 30, 15 days), bank account details, and custom terms.
  6. Export Watermark-Free PDF: Click "Generate High-Res Proposal" to preview your finished document and download an unwatermarked, print-ready PDF invoice directly to your device.
