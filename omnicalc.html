Flagship Modules & Verified Formulas

1. Loan EMI & Mortgage Amortization

When planning a home mortgage, automobile loan, or personal credit facility, calculating the exact Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is essential for budget forecasting. OmniCalc uses standard financial amortization mathematics:

The Mathematical EMI Formula:
E = P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1)

Where:
• E = Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)
• P = Principal Loan Amount borrowed
• r = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
• n = Total Tenor in Months (Years × 12)

In addition to computing the exact monthly payment, OmniCalc renders a dynamic HTML5 Canvas donut visualization displaying the proportional split between Principal repaid and Total Interest incurred over the loan lifecycle.

2. Multi-Currency Exchange Engine

OmniCalc tracks cross-currency valuations between major international fiat currencies including USD, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, CAD, AUD, and AED, allowing instant conversion with transparent decimal precision.

3. High-Precision Unit Conversion

Converting between Imperial and Metric measurements often leads to rounding errors. OmniCalc standardizes conversions using international physical constants across:

  • Length & Distance: Meters, Kilometers, Centimeters, Millimeters, Miles, Yards, Feet, Inches, and Nautical Miles.
  • Weight & Mass: Kilograms, Grams, Milligrams, Metric Tons, Pounds (lbs), Ounces (oz), and Stones.
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin scales with exact thermodynamic offsets.
  • Digital Storage: Bits, Bytes, Kilobytes (KB), Megabytes (MB), Gigabytes (GB), and Terabytes (TB) with binary (1024) and decimal (1000) base accuracy.

4. Health & Fitness Calculations

OmniCalc features clinical health estimation algorithms such as the Body Mass Index (BMI = kg / m²) according to World Health Organization (WHO) classifications, and Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) using the clinically validated Mifflin-St Jeor equation.
