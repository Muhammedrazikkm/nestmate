The Nestmate Story

About Nestmate Group

Crafting high-precision, privacy-preserving utility software for the global web.

Our Vision & Why We Built Nestmate

The modern web is full of barriers. When someone simply wants to generate a clean commercial invoice, create a QR code for their Wi-Fi router, or calculate an accurate home loan amortization schedule, they are routinely confronted with:

  • Mandatory registration forms asking for phone numbers and work emails.
  • Intrusive watermarks plastered across exported PDFs and QR images unless they purchase a premium monthly plan.
  • Unnecessary cloud uploads of sensitive personal and financial spreadsheets to untrusted remote databases.
  • Hidden subscription traps that start with a "free trial".

Nestmate Group was founded in 2026 to dismantle these barriers. We believe that everyday digital utilities—tools that people rely on for work, school, and daily life—should be 100% free, instantaneously accessible, and strictly respectful of human privacy.

Our Three Core Engineering Pillars

1. Privacy by Physics (Local Execution)

We don't just promise privacy through legal legalese; we guarantee it through our software architecture. Every quotation, barcode render, and scientific calculation executes locally inside your web browser. If you disconnect your internet connection while using our tools, they continue working without interruption.

2. Commercial-Grade Quality Without Watermarks

Every document produced in QuoteCraft is completely watermark-free, cleanly formatted, and ready for commercial accounting. Every QR code created in QR Studio complies with ISO/IEC 18004 standards and passes WCAG color contrast checks. We refuse to compromise on design or utility.

3. Zero Gated Access & Open Sustainability

You will never see a "Create an Account to Download" modal on Nestmate. To support our ongoing server hosting and engineering overhead without charging users, we display non-intrusive advertisements powered by Google AdSense.

Our Tool Ecosystem

Nestmate currently hosts three flagship productivity platforms:

  • QuoteCraft: A modular proposal document designer and quotation maker with multi-currency math, automatic tax handling (GST, VAT), itemized discounts, and clean PDF generation.
  • QR Studio: A custom barcode and matrix code generator supporting 10 distinct types (Wi-Fi, vCard, WhatsApp, URL, Crypto) with SVG/PNG exports.
  • OmniCalc: A computational engine offering 78+ verified mathematical, financial, unit, and scientific calculators.

Our Team & Community Pledge

Nestmate is maintained by an independent group of engineers and designers passionate about building public digital goods. We welcome user feedback, bug reports, and suggestions for new tools. If you have an idea for a daily utility you'd love to see on Nestmate, email our development desk anytime.
